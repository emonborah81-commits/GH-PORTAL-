Privacy & Student Data Protection Policy

Rev. William Ward Senior Secondary School — Green House

1. Purpose of This Portal

The Green House Sports & Cultural Participation Portal is an official school coordination tool built exclusively to organize student participation in inter-house sports and cultural activities at Rev. William Ward Senior Secondary School.

2. Data Minimization — What We Collect

To respect student privacy, this portal collects only the minimum details necessary to coordinate house teams:

  • Student Full Name: Used to prepare team rosters and event participation lists.
  • School Roll Number: Used to uniquely identify each student and prevent duplicate entries.
  • Selected Sports & Cultural Events: Used to assign participants to their chosen competitions.
  • Submission Timestamp: Recorded automatically for administrative auditing.

We do not collect home addresses, personal phone numbers, Aadhaar/government IDs, passwords, or payment details from students.

3. Confidentiality & Access Control

Student registration records are never displayed publicly on the website. Database Row Level Security (RLS) ensures that anonymous visitors cannot read, search, edit, or download any student submissions. Only authorized Green House teachers and coordinators verified through authenticated management accounts may view participant lists or export CSV rosters for school event administration.

4. Data Retention & Corrections

Registration records are retained only for the duration of the current academic session's sports and cultural events. If a student needs to correct an event selection or remove an entry, they may contact the Green House Master or House Coordinators directly.
