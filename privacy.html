Privacy Information

GREEN HOUSE — Sports & Cultural Participation Portal

This portal is intended to coordinate school sports and cultural participation.

Information collected

The portal may ask participants for basic information such as name, roll number and selected activities. Do not submit unrelated personal information.

Use and access

Information should be used only for organising participation and should be visible only to authorised school/house management. Access controls and database policies must be properly configured before real student data is entered.

Retention and contact

Management should retain records only as long as needed for school activities and provide a school-approved contact for privacy questions. This template must be reviewed and adapted by the school before use.

This page is a starter template, not a legal privacy notice.
